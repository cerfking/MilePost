# Credits

## Video

The three video clips in `Milepost/Video/` are excerpts from NASA Goddard Space
Flight Center productions. NASA media is generally not copyrighted and may be
used for informational and educational purposes; see
<https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/> for NASA's current
usage guidelines. NASA does not endorse this project, and no NASA insignia or
logos are used.

Each clip was trimmed to a short excerpt and re-encoded (H.264, AAC) for size.
Poster frames in `Assets.xcassets` are single frames extracted from the same
clips.

| File | NASA identifier | Source title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `clip-orbit.mp4` | `GSFC_20150420_Orbit_m11858_2014` | Earth from Orbit 2014 |
| `clip-airglow.mp4` | `GSFC_20180105_GOLD_m12817_Airglow_Milkyway` | Why NASA Is Exploring The Edge Of Our Planet: Airglow |
| `clip-glow.mp4` | `GSFC_20181022_ICON_m12902_Airglow` | The Secrets behind Earth's Multi-colored Glow |

Originals are available from the NASA Image and Video Library at
<https://images.nasa.gov>.

## Audio

The four spoken-word episodes in `Milepost/Audio/` were written for this project
and synthesised locally with the macOS `say` command. They are original to this
repository.

## Artwork

The app icon was generated procedurally with Core Graphics
(`Scripts/makeicon.swift`).
